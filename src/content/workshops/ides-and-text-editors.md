You probably already use an IDE or text editor to write code, but are you making the most of it?

In this workshop, we'll explore the features that text editors like VS Code, IDEs like PyCharm, and modal editors like Vim have to offer, and how you can incorporate them into your development workflow to work faster and more efficiently.

We'll cover practical tools and techniques for **code navigation, keyboard shortcuts, refactoring, debugging, and customization**, helping you get more out of the tools you already use.

The goal isn't to convince you to switch editors or memorize hundreds of shortcuts. It's to help you understand what your tools are capable of and develop better habits that save time when writing software.

**This is a hands-on workshop**, so bring your personal laptop and be ready to follow along, experiment, and improve your workflow.
