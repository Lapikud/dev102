The terminal might look like a mysterious black screen, but it's actually one of the most powerful tools at your disposal. Have you ever wondered what happens behind the scenes when you run a command? What a shell actually is?

In this workshop, we'll explore the **fundamentals of operating systems, why and how Windows, macOS, and Linux differ, and how shells allow us to interact with our computers beyond the graphical interface.**

We'll cover practical tools and techniques such as **navigating the filesystem, working with commands, pipes, shortcuts, and aliases, helping you automate repetitive tasks and make your development workflow faster and more efficient.** 

Like the previous sections of the DEV102 series, the goal isn't to make you memorize hundreds of commands or abandon graphical interfaces. It's to help you understand your operating system, become comfortable with the command line, and discover how much you can accomplish with just a few commands. 

**This is a hands-on workshop**, so bring your personal laptop (Windows, macOS, or Linux) and be ready to follow along and experiment!
